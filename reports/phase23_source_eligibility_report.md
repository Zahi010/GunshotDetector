## 1. Principles of Negative Audio Eligibility

A foundational scientific error in acoustic gunshot detection research is assuming that an audio recording is "negative" simply because it lacks an explicit gunshot label. In reality, uncurated environmental recordings, public surveillance streams, or crowdsourced audio may contain unannotated acoustic transients, commercial fireworks, industrial explosions, or unrecognized ballistic discharges.

Phase 23 enforces a rigorous, 5-category eligibility classification across all candidate streams:

1. `VERIFIED_NEGATIVE`: Supported by rigorous institutional provenance, verified quiet municipal context, or acoustic forensic inspection establishing the absolute absence of firearms discharges throughout the entire recording duration. Only this category enters the primary negative exposure denominator.
2. `POTENTIALLY_NEGATIVE_UNVERIFIED`: Lacks gunshot annotations, but absence of target events cannot be independently corroborated by external sensor or visual ground truth. Retained exclusively in exploratory diagnostic sets.
3. `EVENT_CONTAINING`: Contains confirmed, annotated, or discovered target gunshot events or high-explosive discharges. Strictly excluded from negative exposure calculations.
4. `AMBIGUOUS`: Contains high-amplitude impulsive transients (e.g., anomalous industrial bangs, unconfirmed fireworks) that cannot be definitively ruled out as non-ballistic without auxiliary sensor data.
5. `INVALID`: Corrupt digital files, clipped front-ends, lossy codec artifacts, or synthetic concatenations that violate acoustic continuity standards.

## 2. Eligibility Audit of Candidate Corpora

### 2.1 The 17 Primary Natural Continuous Streams (`VERIFIED_NEGATIVE`)
- **UK Industrial & Environmental Monitoring Suite (Zenodo 7493820):**  
  10 unbroken streams totaling **5.500 hours (19,800.0 seconds)**. Recorded using continuous Raspberry Pi sound monitoring stations in industrial facilities, transit corridors, and public spaces in the United Kingdom. Provenance confirms uninterrupted machine logging during routine non-conflict operations. Spectrogram audits show natural noise floors (air compressors, pumps, ventilation, road traffic) with zero ballistic transients. Classified as `VERIFIED_NEGATIVE`.
- **Chew Magna Reservoir Soundscape (Zenodo 3938837):**  
  1 unbroken stream totaling **0.583 hours (2,100.0 seconds)**. Continuous ecological field recording in a rural UK reservoir. Documented absence of human hunting or firearms activity. Continuous aquatic and avian acoustic background. Classified as `VERIFIED_NEGATIVE`.
- **Bilbao Old Town Control Points (Zenodo 18561165 / 18716205):**  
  6 unbroken streams totaling **0.563 hours (2,025.6 seconds)**. University of the Basque Country acoustic characterization of historic Old Town Bilbao. Multi-point sound level meter and audio logging during peaceful civic activity. Spectrogram review confirms dense urban ambient background (crowds, footsteps, cafe terraces, covered arcades) with zero ballistic transients. Classified as `VERIFIED_NEGATIVE`.
- **Primary Benchmark Total:** **17 streams = 6.646 hours (23,925.58 seconds) of 100% `VERIFIED_NEGATIVE` continuous audio.**

### 2.2 Historical Synthetic Overlay Streams (`POTENTIALLY_NEGATIVE_UNVERIFIED`)
- **Phase 7 & Phase 8 Continuous Streams (6 Streams = 6.000 Hours):**  
  Auditing of `scripts/acquire_phase7_independent_dataset.py` and `scripts/acquire_phase8_final_dataset.py` revealed that these 1-hour streams were generated by inserting discrete ESC-50 clips (horns, rain, bells, machinery) over digital silence.
  - While all inserted sounds are known non-gunshot classes, the streams **lack a natural continuous background noise floor** and exhibit intermittent zero-amplitude gaps.
  - In strict compliance with Section 4 of the prompt, these streams are categorized as `POTENTIALLY_NEGATIVE_UNVERIFIED` (Synthetic Overlay) and are **strictly excluded from the primary natural negative exposure denominator**. They are retained solely as an exploratory synthetic stress-test suite.

### 2.3 SONYC-UST and DCASE Repositories (`INVALID` for Continuous Benchmark)
- **SONYC-UST V1 & V2:** Zenodo records 3338310 and 3966543 consist exclusively of **discrete 10-second clips**. Splicing them together would introduce ~18,500 artificial boundary transients, which Phase 15 proved causes dozens of spurious alerts. Classified as `INVALID` for continuous stream benchmarking.
- **TAU Urban Acoustic Scenes (DCASE):** Pre-segmented into 10-second segments. Classified as `INVALID` for continuous stream benchmarking.
