📋 Protocol for Manual Live Microphone Verification

  1. Setup & Permission: Select your input device and click Start Microphone. Confirm browser microphone permission prompt.
  2. Ambient Noise Floor: In a quiet room, verify the level meter reads ≤ -40 dBFS and FSM state remains strictly in IDLE.
  3. Speech / Continuous Noise: Speak normally or whistle into the microphone. Verify score remains ≤ 0.30 and does NOT trigger state progression.
  4. Transient Impulse Test: Produce a sharp acoustic impulse (e.g., sharp handclap or snap). Observe instantaneous rise in Crest Factor (≥ 12 dB) and transition to SUSPECTED or CONFIRMED.
  5. Refractory Cooldown: After confirmation, verify state enters REFRACTORY_COOLDOWN for 2.5s to prevent false alert chatter.
  6. Latency & Reliability: Confirm chunk latency remains ≤ 25 ms and Dropped Windows remains 0.
