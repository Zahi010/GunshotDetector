## 2. Research Background & Institutional Intent

The requesting research team is conducting non-operational academic machine-learning research into urban acoustic transient classification and false-alarm suppression in environmental monitoring systems.

### 2.1 Core Non-Operational Declaration
1. **Academic Demonstration Prototype Only:** The research system is strictly an experimental machine-learning prototype. It is not a commercial product, is not certified for life safety, and will never be connected to emergency dispatch, 911 computer-aided dispatch (CAD), or tactical security infrastructure.
2. **No Hazardous or Live-Fire Testing:** The project evaluates pre-recorded audio playback only. No weapons discharges, live-fire testing, or explosive acoustic testing are requested, performed, or permitted.
3. **No Personally Identifiable Information (PII) Desired:** The research focuses exclusively on non-speech environmental acoustic background noise (e.g. traffic tire-road noise, wind, rain, industrial ventilation, structural machinery). Any accidental speech intelligibility is undesirable and will be permanently masked or discarded.

## 3. Specifications of Requested Audio Data

To facilitate scientific replication without imposing operational burdens on the host institution, the request specifies the following technical parameters:

| Parameter | Desired Specification | Minimum Acceptable Specification |
|---|---|---|
| **Acoustic Environment** | Urban streetscapes, transit corridors, public plazas, commercial parks | Suburban arterial, industrial yard, or mixed-use perimeter |

| **Audio Format** | Uncompressed WAV or lossless FLAC (Linear PCM) | Uncompressed PCM, 16-bit or 24-bit |

| **Provenance Metadata** | Sensor model, general neighborhood, height above ground, date/time | General municipality/climate zone and sensor class |

## 4. Privacy, Security & Data Handling Safeguards

The research team formally commits to the following data handling standards upon receipt of any collaboration data:

1. **Secure Storage:** All received audio will be stored on encrypted local volumes with restricted access, isolated from public internet facing servers.
2. **Strict Non-Redistribution:** No raw audio recordings will be republished, open-sourced, mirrored, or redistributed to third parties without prior written consent from the providing institution.
3. **Privacy Compliance & Speech Scrubbing:** If human conversation or private speech is identified within any continuous recording interval, that interval will be scrubbed, excluded from publication, and permanently redacted from evaluation logs.
4. **Citation & Attribution:** Full scholarly attribution and institutional citation will be provided in all resulting technical reports and open publications, following the provider's preferred citation format.
5. **Data Destruction on Request:** The providing institution retains the right to request deletion of transferred audio at any point during or following the study.
